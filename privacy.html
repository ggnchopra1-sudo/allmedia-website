Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

All Media & Marketing ("we", "us") runs allmediain.in and its advertising campaigns, including lead forms on Facebook and Instagram. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Details you choose to give us: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email, city, and what you tell us about your business or project.
  • Basic, non-identifying website analytics, such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it

  • To contact you by WhatsApp, phone call or email about the enquiry you made.
  • To prepare a quote, proposal or consultation for your project.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with team members who need it to respond to you, or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and manage our business relationship with you, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you, or to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Just message us on WhatsApp and we'll take care of it.
