The brief

IPSA is a jewellery brand built as a tribute to India's many regions: the opulence of Rajasthan, the minimal charm of Kerala, the artistry of Gujarat and the bold tribal patterns of the Northeast. Each piece carries a story.

We built an identity to match: quiet, luxurious and rooted in heritage, yet modern enough for a new generation who wear their culture with pride.

The story

"We don't just create jewellery; we craft stories, celebrate traditions, and carry forward the art of India's past into the future."

Inspired by heritage

From Odisha's filigree to Rajasthan's enamel and South Indian temple jewellery, the brand draws on India's festivals, rituals and craftsmanship, blended with modern design.

A monogram that's also a jewel

The mark draws the founder Dherya Popli's initials as one continuous line, shaped like a pendant. Subtle, luxurious and easy to recognise, it leads the IPSA wordmark and works alone on packaging and pattern.
